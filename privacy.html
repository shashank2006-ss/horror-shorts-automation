Privacy Policy

Application: Horror Shorts Automation

Last updated: November 13, 2026

1. Overview

Horror Shorts Automation is a personal project used by its owner to generate and publish original Hindi horror and suspense videos on YouTube. It is not offered as a public service to other users.

2. Information and services used

The application uses Google OAuth and the YouTube Data API to authorize video uploads to the owner's YouTube channel. It may process video titles, descriptions, narration, generated content and channel authorization credentials.

Google Gemini is used to generate story content. Pixabay is used to search for stock footage. Microsoft Edge Text to Speech is used to generate narration audio.

3. How information is used

Information is processed only to generate stories, create videos and upload them to the owner's YouTube channel. OAuth credentials are stored in the local n8n automation environment and are used to authorize API requests.

4. Data sharing

Content and information are sent to the third-party services necessary for the application's operation, including Google, Pixabay and Microsoft's text-to-speech service. YouTube receives videos and associated metadata when uploads are performed.

5. Data storage and security

Generated videos, narration audio and workflow data are stored locally on the owner's computer. Reasonable measures are taken to protect local files and credentials. No system can guarantee absolute security.

6. Data retention and deletion

Locally stored project files may be deleted by the owner. OAuth access can be revoked through the owner's Google Account security settings. Data handled by third-party services is subject to their respective retention policies.

7. Children's privacy

This application is not designed to collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the application's functionality or data handling practices change.
